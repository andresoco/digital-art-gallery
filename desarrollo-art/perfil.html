Artist in Residence

Elias
Vanguard

Contemporary Abstract

"My work seeks to capture the silent tension between architectural geometry and the chaotic nature of human emotion. Each canvas is a structured rebellion."

Emerging from the brutalist structures of modern urban environments, Vanguard’s recent collections explore the dichotomy of hard lines and soft shadows. His monochromatic approach strips away the distraction of color, forcing the viewer to confront form and negative space.
