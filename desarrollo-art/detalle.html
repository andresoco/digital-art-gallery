Ethereal Constructs

Elara Vance, 2024

An exploration of negative space and digital fragility. Vance's latest work challenges the perception of permanence in the digital age, utilizing procedural generation to create forms that feel both organic and fundamentally synthetic.

Technical Details

  • Medium: Algorithmic Projection on Canvas
  • Dimensions: 120 x 180 cm
  • Edition: Unique Work (1 of 1)
